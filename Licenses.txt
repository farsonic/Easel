Changes made to dav1d for Easel: built for one thread without assembly (av1/config.h,
av1/compat/), anonymous structs and unions given names so vbcc can compile it
(av1/tools/name_anon.py does this), and small vbcc fixes in attributes.h, cdf.c and itx_tmpl.c.

Easel needs MUI (muimaster.library) to run. MUI is not part of Easel and is not included; it has
its own licence from its authors.
